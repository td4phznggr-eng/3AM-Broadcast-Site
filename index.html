3AM Broadcast

Short-form story videos for the hour when you should be asleep.

3AM Broadcast is a media studio producing original short-form story videos across four genres — horror, mystery, drama, and true crime — published daily to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. Every video is a self-contained story: a hook, a turn, and an ending built for viewers who watch to the end.

What we publish

  • Horror — unexplained phenomena, cosmic dread, things found where they shouldn't be.
  • Mystery — puzzles, disappearances, and the moment a small detail changes everything.
  • Drama — confessions, family secrets, and the choices people make under pressure.
  • True crime — real cases, told with care for the people involved.
